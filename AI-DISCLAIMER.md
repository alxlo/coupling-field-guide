# Use of AI

This field guide is my work. The model of four origins of coupling and the method from symptom to owner come from my experience as an architect and trainer; the guide grew out of my talk *Where Coupling Comes From* (Software Architecture Gathering 2026). I decided what goes on the card and how it is laid out, checked the sources by hand and tested the guide in print.

I worked with an AI assistant, Claude by Anthropic, as sparring partner and research assistant. It challenged my ideas, read the literature with me, traced claims back to their primary sources, drafted wording and wrote the script that sets the pages. That help was substantial.

The guide cites only the sources listed in the [README](README.md#sources). I publish it under my name and take responsibility for it; any error in it is mine. If you find one, please open an issue.
